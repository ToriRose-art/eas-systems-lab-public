# AI-Assisted Application Clarity Policy

## Core policy statement

> **AI-assisted applications:**  
> AI tools are [welcome / allowed with limitations / allowed with disclosure / not permitted] for this application. Applicants remain responsible for the accuracy, truthfulness, and final review of their submission.
>
> This policy does not replace identity, eligibility, employment, or security verification. Any required verification will occur through the organization’s designated process.

## Policy options

- **AI welcome:** AI assistance is allowed.
- **AI allowed with limitations:** AI may be used only for specified purposes.
- **AI allowed with disclosure:** Applicants must explain how AI was used.
- **AI not permitted:** Applicants must complete the specified portion without AI assistance.
- **Policy not specified:** The organization has not stated a position.

## Safety boundary

This statement communicates AI-use expectations for an application. It is not an identity-verification badge, security certification, or guarantee that any applicant or organization is legitimate. Organizations remain responsible for their own verification and security processes.

## Privacy

If an organization records AI use, it should explain what is recorded, why it is recorded, who can access it, how long it is retained, whether it affects review, and how applicants can ask questions or request correction.
