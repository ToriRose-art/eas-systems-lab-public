# Optional Applicant Attestation Pack

These are optional extensions. They are not identity verification and should not require applicants to upload identification into an AI tool.

## Human applicant attestation

☐ **I confirm that I am the human applicant behind this submission.**

## Verification willingness

☐ **If requested, I agree to provide accurate identity, location, work-authorization, or eligibility information through the organization’s designated secure verification process.**

These attestations do not replace interviews, references, background checks, eligibility checks, or other verification.
